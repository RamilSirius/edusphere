How learning works

Five steps from sign-up to certificate

  1. Create a free account

    Sign up in a minute — your saved courses and reading list stay in your profile.

  2. Choose a course and a format

    Evening, weekend or intensive — switch once per module if your week changes.

  3. Join live classes

    Ninety minutes, twice a week, with questions welcome at any moment.

  4. Get feedback within 24 hours

    Your mentor comments every practice task right in the code.

  5. Defend your final project

    Present it to the group and receive a verifiable certificate.
