How to read the timetable

  • Group A / BThe same course twice — pick the slot that fits your evening.

  • ConsultationsAn open room where any student can join and ask questions.
  • Open lectureA guest talk, free even without a subscription.
Every lesson

Joining a live class

  1. Open the student portal 10 minutes before the start.
  2. Click the room link on the card of your group.
  3. Turn the microphone on when you want to ask — interrupting is welcome.
  4. Download the notes and start the practice task the same evening.
