About EduSphere

Practice first, theory second

On most platforms a student watches for ten hours and writes code for one. We turn that around: every module is one short theory block and three practical tasks checked by a mentor.

Our mission

Make practice the centre of the course, not a bonus

No one moves on to the next module until the mentor has accepted the work. It is slower in the first week and much faster by the last one — because nothing is left half-understood.

Our students are first-year university students, career changers and school leavers in Kazakhstan who want a portfolio they can show, not a pile of watched videos.

Real projects

Every course ends with something you built yourself.

Live teaching

Questions are answered in the class, not in a forum a week later.

Honest feedback

Mentors comment the code line by line within 24 hours.

Open to everyone

A student discount and a free first class in every course.
